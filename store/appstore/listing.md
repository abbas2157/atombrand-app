### Description (4000)

Atombrand is the partner app for brands selling on AtomShop, Pakistan's fastest-growing Buy Now, Pay Later marketplace.

If your brand is listed on atomshop.pk, this is where you run it. See every order the moment it lands, move it through fulfilment, answer bulk enquiries while the lead is still warm, and keep your products, stock and storefront current — without waiting on anyone else.

ORDERS, RETAIL AND INSTALMENT
Separate feeds for retail and instalment orders, with status filters and search. Open an order for the full item list, totals and buyer contact details, then call or message them directly.

FULFILMENT WITH PROOF
Advance an order through its stages and close it with a proof-of-delivery photo and the name of whoever signed for it, captured on the spot.

BULK ENQUIRIES
Bulk leads arrive tabbed by stage with live counts, so nothing sits unanswered.

CATALOGUE AND INVENTORY
Add and edit products, upload images, and keep stock levels accurate from your phone.

YOUR BRAND PAGE
Edit the storefront customers see on AtomShop: your logo, banner and the slides that tell your story.

AT A GLANCE
The dashboard opens on what changed — catalogue and order counts, open bulk requests, top products and your latest orders.

WHO IT'S FOR
Atombrand is for approved AtomShop brand partners: manufacturers, authorised importers and distributors. It is not a shopping app. You need a partner account, which our team issues once your brand is approved.

New to AtomShop? Apply from the welcome screen and we'll reply within two business days.

### Notes (4000)

Atombrand is a business tool for brands that sell on AtomShop (atomshop.pk), a marketplace in Pakistan. It is not a consumer shopping app.

Access requires a partner account issued by our team after a brand is approved; there is no public self-registration. Please sign in with the demo credentials above. That account is seeded with sample orders, products and bulk enquiries so every screen can be exercised.

Suggested walkthrough:
1. Sign in with the demo account.
2. Dashboard — counts, top products, latest orders.
3. Orders tab — switch between Retail and Instalment, open any order.
4. Bulk tab — bulk enquiries by stage.
5. Catalogue tab — Products and Inventory.
6. More tab — brand page editor and profile.

Camera and photo library access are used for exactly two things: attaching a proof-of-delivery photo when completing an order, and uploading product images, brand logo and banner. There is no background location, tracking or advertising SDK in the app.

Customer contact details visible in the order screens are sample data on the demo account.
