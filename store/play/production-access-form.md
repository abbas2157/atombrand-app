## 1. About your closed test

### How did you recruit users for your closed test? For example, did you ask friends and family, or use a paid testing provider?

We contacted brands that already sell on AtomShop, along with members of our own team, and sent each of them the Play opt-in link over WhatsApp, which is how we normally deal with our partners. Twelve testers opted in and stayed on the test.

### How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?

**Neither difficult or easy**

### Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test

*Include whether or not testers utilized all of the features in your app, and whether tester usage was consistent with how you would expect a real user to use your app. If not, describe the differences you would expect to see.*

Testers used a shared partner account, as accounts are issued manually by our team rather than self-registered. They signed in on their own devices and worked through the dashboard, orders and catalogue. A real partner uses their own account and returns to orders daily.

### Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers. Include how you collected the feedback.

We collected feedback over WhatsApp and in direct conversation with the testers. The app worked as expected on their own devices. The points raised were small ones about wording and appearance rather than faults, and we dealt with them before release.

## 2. About your app

### Who is the intended audience of your app?

AtomShop brand partners in Pakistan: manufacturers, authorised importers and distributors whose products are listed on atomshop.pk. The user is the person at the brand who handles orders and stock, not a shopper. Accounts are issued by our team once a brand is approved, so it isn't for the public.

### Describe how your app provides value to users

Partners used to chase their AtomShop orders by phone and spreadsheet. The app puts it in one place: retail and instalment orders, delivery status with a proof photo, bulk enquiries, products and stock levels, and their public brand page, all editable by them without going through our staff.

### How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?

**0 - 10K**

---

## 3. Your production readiness

### What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?

We dropped Sign in with Google. Partners get their credentials from our team, so a second sign-in route only caused confusion. We also replaced the default Flutter icon and splash with our own branding and set the app name to Atombrand, so it is recognisable on a partner's home screen.

### How did you decide that your app is ready for production?

Twelve testers used the app on their own devices across two rounds of closed testing without reporting crashes or blocking problems. The main flows were exercised against our live server, and we are satisfied the app is stable enough for a wider release.

---

## 4. Additional testing

### What did you do differently this time?

Our first test had not run fourteen continuous days with twelve testers opted in. This time we kept the same twelve on the track for a full uninterrupted cycle, with a partner account available so they could sign in and use the app rather than only install it.
